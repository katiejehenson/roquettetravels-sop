Good to Know

8 notes

Workflow emails are copies

Each workflow automation keeps its own copy of the subject and body. Editing a template in the library doesn’t change the copy inside a workflow, so update both when wording changes.

Disney Cruise workflows are off

DCL First Timers and DCL Streamlined have no auto-apply and every automation is inactive, so their 25 DCL templates only send once the workflow is applied and the automations are turned on. DCL 09 (booking window) has no day offset set yet.

Land & Cruise isn’t running

Auto-apply is off and all four emails are inactive. Its Visa + Health email uses slightly different wording from the library template.

Trip Review email

Inactive in the New and Existing Client workflows but active in the Trip Status workflow, which applies to every new trip, so clients get it once, 22 days before travel.

Passport timing

The Passport template says the passport expires “in about 200 days,” but the Passport Expiration automation sends it 150 days before expiry.

Emails with no library template

“Thank you for your payment!” (All Trips Payments), “Bon Voyage!” (Cruise Booked, inactive) and the five Universal Studios Florida emails (inactive) were written directly in their workflows.

Similar templates

Old/new pairs to review: the four Insurance Quote templates, Tern Account vs Quote In Progress (same subject), Disney Cruise Line Deposit (2)/Final Payment vs DCL Deposit Paid MC/Paid in Full MC. WDW 10.0 and 10.5 share a subject but serve different booking windows.

Fill-ins to check

Royal Caribbean – Deposit Confirmation has XXXX placeholders, WDW 13 has a blank (_____), and Polite Decline (at capacity) opens “Hi E,” instead of a name variable.
