Good to know

Friendly flags from the review. Nothing here was changed in Tern.

Workflow emails are copies

Each Workflow automation keeps its own copy of the subject and body. Editing a template in Email Templates doesn't change the copy inside a Workflow, so update both when wording changes.

Two Disney Cruise Line Workflows are switched off

DCL First Timers and DCL Streamlined have no Auto-Apply and every automation is Inactive, so their 25 DCL templates only send if you apply the Workflow and turn the automations on. The Booking Window automation (DCL 09) has no day offset set yet.

Land & Cruise isn't running

Land & Cruise (Tag = Land & Sea) has Auto-Apply off and all four of its emails Inactive. Its Visa + Health email has slightly different wording from the library template.

Trip Review email

“Trip Review email to book time” is Inactive in the New and Existing Client Workflows but Active in the Trip Status Workflow, which applies to every new trip. Clients get it once, 22 days out.

Passport timing

The Passport template says the passport expires “in about 200 days”, but the Passport Expiration automation sends it 150 days before expiry.

Emails with no library template

A few Workflow emails were written directly in the Workflow: “Thank you for your payment!” (All Trips Payments), “Bon Voyage!” (Cruise Booked, Inactive) and the five Universal Studios Florida emails (all Inactive). The Universal Orlando 00 template isn’t used by that Workflow.

Similar templates worth a look

Possible old/new pairs: the four Insurance Quote templates (two Travel Insured, two cruise versions); Tern Account and Quote In Progress share a subject; Disney Cruise Line Deposit (2)/Final Payment vs the newer DCL Deposit Paid MC/Paid in Full MC; WDW 10.0 and 10.5 share a subject but suit different booking windows. A duplicate review would confirm which to keep.

Fill-ins to check before sending

Royal Caribbean - Deposit Confirmation has XXXX placeholders, WDW 13 has a blank (_____), and Polite Decline (at capacity) opens “Hi E,” rather than a name variable.

Quiet stages

Nothing sends automatically during Inquiry & lead or Research & proposal; those emails (declines, proposal, insurance quotes) are manual. During Traveling only FIT - Arrival Day Check-in sends.

Not in this catalog

The Destinations folder is left out as requested, along with the Workflows that send destination content (London Trip and the B&B destination Workflows).

Key takeaway

Your core client journey (new and existing client planning, payments, FIT, cruise and Walt Disney World) is fully automated from the Automated emails and WDW folders. The biggest opportunities are the two Disney Cruise Line Workflows and Land & Cruise, which are built but switched off, and a tidy-up of the older duplicate templates so the right version is always the one you grab.
